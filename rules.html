🛡️ Cheating Policy

If a player is suspected of cheating, BSCL administrators may request:

  • MOSS recording
  • Live gameplay stream

📢 Reporting Loss

You must wait until you return to the lobby before reporting a match result.

⚠️ Dodging & Leaving Games

Using the /subme command because you think your team is bad, or leaving during a match, will result in penalties.

Penalty System

  • Below 1000 ELO → -50 points
  • 1000 - 1500 ELO → -100 points
  • 1500 - 2000 ELO → -150 points
  • 2000 - 3000 ELO → -200 points
  • Above 3000 ELO → -250 points

Penalties are applied for every single dodge.

Leaving a game before it finishes because you believe your team will lose will result in a -100 ELO penalty.

🔄 Remake Rules

Only ONE remake per team is allowed.

Example:

  • Team 1 / Player 1 crashes → Team 1 can remake.
  • After Team 1 has used its remake, no second remake will be granted.
  • If Team 2 experiences a crash, Team 2 may still use its own remake.

📣 Final Reminder

Respect all BSCL rules and maintain fair play at all times.
